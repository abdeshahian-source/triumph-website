What It Is

A treatment program that fits the workflow.

Workers' compensation and motor vehicle accident cases require timely treatment, careful documentation, and direct coordination with case managers, adjusters, employers, and sometimes attorneys. Care that ignores those realities — however clinically sound — fails the patient. Triumph runs a structured intake, treatment, and reporting workflow specifically for these cases.

How It Works

Same diagnostic and interventional toolkit. Built-in case coordination.

The clinical care looks like the rest of what we offer — precise diagnosis, image-guided interventions, regenerative options where appropriate, structured PT, and surgical referral when warranted. What is added is structured reporting, work-status documentation at every visit, direct communication with the case manager, and — when requested — independent medical examinations and functional capacity evaluations.

Who It's For

For acute and chronic work injuries and post-MVA presentations.

Common presentations include lumbar and cervical strains from lifting and slip-and-fall injuries, whiplash and post-concussive presentations from motor vehicle collisions, shoulder and knee injuries from occupational overuse, and chronic pain syndromes following surgical management. We accept new referrals from primary care, urgent care, employers, attorneys, and case managers.

Recovery & Results

Return to work tracked as an explicit outcome.

Work status — full duty, restricted duty, off — is documented at every visit. Restrictions are specific (lifting limit, position, hours). The goal is the safest return to the highest function the underlying injury permits. We support that with the full Triumph treatment menu and with the documentation the case requires.
