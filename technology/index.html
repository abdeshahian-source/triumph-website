Diagnostic precision

Diagnose to the millimeter before we treat.

Every Triumph procedure starts with an exam by a physician who treats this exact problem every day — plus the imaging and electrodiagnostic tools to confirm the answer.

High-resolution MRI review

We read your imaging side-by-side with you. Sub-millimeter resolution where the pain generator lives — not just where it radiates.

Diagnostic musculoskeletal ultrasound

Real-time, in-clinic imaging of tendons, ligaments, joints, and peripheral nerves. No second visit, no second referral.

EMG / NCV

Electrodiagnostic studies to localize nerve involvement — radiculopathy, neuropathy, entrapments — with objective data, not guesswork.

Targeted diagnostic blocks

When imaging is ambiguous, we use precision blocks to confirm the pain source before any definitive procedure.

Procedural technology

Every procedure, image-guided.

If we can’t see the target in real time, we don’t treat it. Image guidance is the standard at every Triumph site for every interventional procedure.

Fluoroscopy (C-arm)

Live X-ray guidance for spine injections, RFA, kyphoplasty, vertiflex, and SI joint procedures. Standard at every Triumph site.

Ultrasound-guided injections

Peripheral joint, tendon, bursa, and nerve procedures performed with continuous real-time visualization. No fluoroscopy radiation exposure when ultrasound is the right tool.

Radiofrequency ablation

Conventional, cooled, and pulsed RFA for facet, sacroiliac, and genicular targets. Single- and multi-needle platforms calibrated per case.

Spinal cord and DRG stimulation

Trial-and-implant workflow for severe neuropathic pain, CRPS, and failed back surgery syndrome. Multi-vendor platform — we pick the device that fits the diagnosis.

Regenerative biologics

Image-guided PRP and bone marrow concentrate procedures for joint, tendon, and ligament targets. Evidence-driven indications only.

MILD & Vertiflex

Minimally invasive lumbar decompression and interspinous spacers for lumbar spinal stenosis — outpatient, same-day, ligament-sparing.
