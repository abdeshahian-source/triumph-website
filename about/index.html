About Triumph

We don't manage pain.
We end it.

Triumph was built around a single conviction: chronic pain is solvable when you bring diagnostic precision, image-guided procedures, and outcomes data into the same operating room.

Home› About

For most patients, chronic pain has become a series of half-measures — a pill, an injection, a physical-therapy referral, another appointment. Triumph is built on the premise that this is not how it has to work.

The standard we hold

Every Triumph physician is board-certified, fellowship-trained, and held to the same outcomes scorecard. We measure first-procedure success, return-to-activity time, complication rates, and patient-reported outcomes on every case — and we publish them annually.

Our patients are athletes, executives, builders, parents, and clinicians themselves. The common thread isn't demographics; it's the refusal to accept that pain is just something you live with.

How we work

  • Precision diagnostics before anything else. Imaging at sub-millimeter resolution, targeted nerve blocks for diagnostic clarity, and a physical exam by a clinician who treats this exact problem all day.
  • Minimally invasive first. Image-guided injections, radiofrequency ablation, spinal cord stimulation, regenerative biologics. Surgery is the final tool, not the first.
  • A quantified recovery plan — every patient receives one. You'll know what good looks like, when to expect it, and what to do if you're not getting there.
  • Transparent cash-pay pricing. No surprise bills. Procedures are priced in advance.

Why the Spartan

The figure you'll see across Triumph's identity isn't decoration. The glowing spine and arm bones visible through the warrior's armor are exactly what we look for in every patient: the underlying structure, made visible, then treated with precision. The Spartan stands for resilience after recovery — the version of you we're trying to give back.
