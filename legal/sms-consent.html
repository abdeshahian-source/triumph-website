Text Message (SMS) Consent

This page reproduces, word for word, the text message consent section that appears on the Triumph Ortho & Spine patient registration form. Patients complete the section in writing at the front desk, or give the same consent verbally to staff when they schedule an appointment by phone.

Triumph Ortho & Spine, LLC — Patient Registration Form, Section 6

Consent to Receive Text Messages

Mobile number: ______________________________________

Yes. I agree to receive text messages from Triumph Ortho & Spine at the mobile number above.

I understand that these messages contain a link to my new patient intake forms and the practice phone number, and that they are sent for treatment and scheduling purposes only. Triumph Ortho & Spine does not send marketing or promotional text messages, and does not include my name, appointment details, diagnosis, or any other health information in a text message.

Message frequency varies and is typically one message per visit. Message and data rates may apply. I can reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or reply HELP for help. I can also call (877) 215-7246 to opt out or to get help.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of receiving care, treatment, or any service from Triumph Ortho & Spine, and declining will not affect my care.

Triumph Ortho & Spine does not sell, rent, or share my mobile number or my consent to receive text messages with any third party for marketing purposes. Mobile numbers are shared only with the telecommunications provider that delivers the message on the practice's behalf, solely for that purpose.

Mobile Terms of Service: triumphorthospine.com/legal/terms.html
Privacy Policy: triumphorthospine.com/legal/privacy.html

Patient signature: ______________________________________     Date: ________________

Printed name: ______________________________________

Verbal consent

When a patient schedules by phone, staff read the same terms aloud before entering the number: what the message contains, that it is about one message per visit, that message and data rates may apply, that the patient can reply STOP to stop or HELP for help or call (877) 215-7246, where the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are found, and that consent is not a condition of care. The patient's yes or no answer is recorded on the registration form.

How a number is entered

Mobile numbers are entered only by practice staff, through an internal passcode-protected tool at triumphorthospine.com/staff. There is no public web form and no online sign-up for these messages.
