Accessibility Statement

Triumph Ortho & Spine is committed to WCAG 2.1 AA conformance.

Home› Accessibility
Draft for attorney review. Last updated May 23, 2026.

Triumph Ortho & Spine LLC is committed to ensuring that our website, triumphorthospine.com, is accessible to people with disabilities. We believe that everyone deserves dignified, equitable access to health information about our practice.

Our Commitment

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines describe how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, language, learning, and neurological disabilities.

Accessibility is treated as an integral part of how we design, build, and update the Website — not a one-time project.

Legal Framework

As a health care provider, we recognize and work to comply with applicable federal accessibility obligations, including:

  • Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, as implemented by U.S. Department of Health and Human Services regulations;
  • Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), prohibiting discrimination on the basis of disability by places of public accommodation;
  • Section 1557 of the Affordable Care Act, prohibiting discrimination on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, or sex in covered health programs and activities.

Measures We Take

To support an accessible experience, we work to:

  • Use semantic HTML and ARIA landmarks where appropriate;
  • Maintain clear visual hierarchy, sufficient color contrast, and readable typography;
  • Provide text alternatives for non-decorative images;
  • Ensure interactive elements are keyboard navigable with visible focus indicators;
  • Include skip-to-main-content links for screen reader users;
  • Test against current versions of common assistive technologies (screen readers and keyboard-only navigation).

Known Limitations

The Website is new as of May 2026. No accessibility barriers have currently been identified. If you encounter a barrier, please let us know using the feedback channel below so we can address it promptly. We will update this section as any limitations are identified and remediated.

Last Audit

Initial internal accessibility review: May 23, 2026. We intend to conduct periodic accessibility reviews and to engage external accessibility auditors as appropriate.

Feedback — We Want to Hear From You

If you experience an accessibility barrier on triumphorthospine.com, or if you need information in an alternative format (large print, audio, accessible PDF, etc.), please contact us. Your feedback helps us improve.

Continuous Improvement

Accessibility is a journey, not a destination. We are committed to ongoing improvement of the Website, to training our team on accessible content practices, and to incorporating accessibility considerations into procurement decisions for third-party tools and services.
